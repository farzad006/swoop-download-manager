## Swoop 1.0.15

### ⬇️ Download

**[Swoop-Setup-x64.exe](https://github.com/farzad006/swoop-download-manager/releases/download/v1.0.15/Swoop-Setup-x64.exe)** for Windows 10/11. Install it over your current version; your downloads and settings are kept.

> Swoop isn't code-signed yet, so Edge may say *"isn't commonly downloaded"* (click **⋯ → Keep → Show more → Keep anyway**) and Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* (click **More info → Run anyway**).

### 🐛 Fixed

- **"The extracted extension ('mp4\"') is unusual"** on some video sites. The video downloader misread players that pages write from a script, so it offered qualities that could never download. Swoop now spots this, reads the page itself and lists the video files it links to, with their sizes and the best quality first. They download with Swoop's own fast multi-connection downloader.
- **More videos found when the video downloader gives up.** Whenever a page can't be read, Swoop now looks for video files linked on the page and offers them under **Video files found on the page**.
