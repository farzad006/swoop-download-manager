## Swoop 1.0.10

### ⬇️ Download

**[Swoop-Setup-x64.exe](https://github.com/farzad006/swoop-download-manager/releases/download/v1.0.10/Swoop-Setup-x64.exe)** for Windows 10/11. Install it over your current version; your downloads and settings are kept.

> Swoop isn't code-signed yet, so Edge may say *"isn't commonly downloaded"* (click **⋯ → Keep → Show more → Keep anyway**) and Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* (click **More info → Run anyway**).

### ⚡ Faster

- **No more waiting on "Looking at the video…".** On many sites the video downloader needs several seconds (longer while other downloads use the connection) to find every quality. The dialog now offers the video your browser is already playing straight away, with a **Download** button, while it keeps looking for the other qualities. Short hover previews are left out, and the file is named after the video's title.
- **Several video tabs opened at once all get looked up in advance.** Swoop used to look up two at a time and skip the rest, so clicking in the others meant a full wait. They now wait their turn.
