## Swoop 1.0.11

### ⬇️ Download

**[Swoop-Setup-x64.exe](https://github.com/farzad006/swoop-download-manager/releases/download/v1.0.11/Swoop-Setup-x64.exe)** for Windows 10/11. Install it over your current version; your downloads and settings are kept.

> Swoop isn't code-signed yet, so Edge may say *"isn't commonly downloaded"* (click **⋯ → Keep → Show more → Keep anyway**) and Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* (click **More info → Run anyway**).

### 🐛 Fixed

- **"Connection was reset" errors** (`curl: (35) Recv failure`) are now retried like other dropped connections.
- **Sites your internet provider blocks** now get a plain explanation instead of a wall of technical text. If a site's connection is cut off every time, it's usually blocked by the provider. Your browser may still show the site through its own VPN (Opera has one built in), but Swoop can't use a browser's VPN. Turn on a VPN for the whole PC (for example Cloudflare WARP in **WARP** mode rather than DNS-only), or set a proxy in **Settings → Advanced**.
