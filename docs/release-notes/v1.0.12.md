## Swoop 1.0.12

### ⬇️ Download

**[Swoop-Setup-x64.exe](https://github.com/farzad006/swoop-download-manager/releases/download/v1.0.12/Swoop-Setup-x64.exe)** for Windows 10/11. Install it over your current version; your downloads and settings are kept.

> Swoop isn't code-signed yet, so Edge may say *"isn't commonly downloaded"* (click **⋯ → Keep → Show more → Keep anyway**) and Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* (click **More info → Run anyway**).

### 🐛 Fixed

- **Videos on flaky connections.** Some internet providers cut off a random share of connections to certain sites. A video lookup makes several connections, so it often failed. Swoop now tries up to 6 times when a connection is cut off.
- **"No video formats found"** when the site's list of files didn't arrive is now treated as a dropped connection and retried.
- **Clearer errors.** When the page can't be read and the video address your browser played doesn't work either (often "HTTP Error 410: Gone", because those addresses only work for the browser), Swoop now shows why the page failed. The "your provider seems to block this site" message only appears when every try was cut off.
