## Swoop 1.0.9

### ⬇️ Download

**[Swoop-Setup-x64.exe](https://github.com/farzad006/swoop-download-manager/releases/download/v1.0.9/Swoop-Setup-x64.exe)** for Windows 10/11. Install it over your current version; your downloads and settings are kept.

> Swoop isn't code-signed yet, so Edge may say *"isn't commonly downloaded"* (click **⋯ → Keep → Show more → Keep anyway**) and Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* (click **More info → Run anyway**).

### 🐛 Fixed

- **Big videos failing at the end with "Postprocessing: Conversion failed!".** Videos from YouTube and similar sites arrive as separate picture and sound files that are joined at the end, so for a moment the video takes up twice its size. When the drive filled up during the join, you only saw that cryptic error, after hours of downloading. Swoop now:
  - checks the space first, and says how much is needed if the drive can't hold the video;
  - explains a join that ran out of space, keeps the downloaded parts (so **Try again** only has to join them) and removes the half-joined file, which can be as big as the video.
