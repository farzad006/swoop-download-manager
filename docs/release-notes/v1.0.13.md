## Swoop 1.0.13

### ⬇️ Download

**[Swoop-Setup-x64.exe](https://github.com/farzad006/swoop-download-manager/releases/download/v1.0.13/Swoop-Setup-x64.exe)** for Windows 10/11. Install it over your current version; your downloads and settings are kept.

> Swoop isn't code-signed yet, so Edge may say *"isn't commonly downloaded"* (click **⋯ → Keep → Show more → Keep anyway**) and Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* (click **More info → Run anyway**).

### 🐛 Fixed

- **"HTTP Error 403: Forbidden" on video pages.** If your browser reached the site through its own VPN (Opera has one built in), the site's cookies only work through that VPN, and the site refuses them from your PC's connection. Swoop now tries again without the browser's cookies when a page is refused.
- **No more tiny previews offered as "Video files found on the page".** Short hover previews (a few hundred KB) are left out, as they already were in the "Don't want to wait?" panel.
