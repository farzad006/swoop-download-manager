## Swoop 1.0.2

### ⬇️ Download

**[Swoop-Setup-x64.exe](https://github.com/farzad006/swoop-download-manager/releases/download/v1.0.2/Swoop-Setup-x64.exe)** for Windows 10/11. Install it over your current version; your downloads and settings are kept. Then reload the browser extension once (on your browser's extensions page, click the reload icon on Swoop) to get the faster video button.

> Swoop isn't code-signed yet, so Edge may say *"isn't commonly downloaded"* (click **⋯ → Keep → Show more → Keep anyway**) and Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* (click **More info → Run anyway**).

### 🐛 Fixed

- **Videos with characters like ’ or – in their title** reached 100% and then showed as failed ("yt-dlp finished without reporting a file"), even though the video was saved. They now finish normally.
- **Videos that were wrongly marked failed are repaired automatically** when you start 1.0.2, as long as the file is on your disk.

### ⚡ Faster "Download this video"

- **Gets ready in advance:** when the *Download this video* button appears, Swoop looks up the video's qualities in the background, so clicking opens the list much sooner. In testing, the list appeared in 1.6 s instead of 3.9 s. Turn it off in Settings → Videos.
- **One-click downloads:** tick *Always download in this quality without asking* in the quality window, or pick a quality in **Settings → Videos → When you click "Download this video"**. The button then starts the download straight away.

### 🔧 Behind the scenes

- The browser connector (`swoop-host.exe`) and the installer now show product name, version and publisher in their file details, ready for code signing.
- GitHub Actions updated to Node.js 24.
