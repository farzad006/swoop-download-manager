## Swoop 1.0.18

### ⬇️ Download

**[Swoop-Setup-x64.exe](https://github.com/farzad006/swoop-download-manager/releases/download/v1.0.18/Swoop-Setup-x64.exe)** for Windows 10/11. Install it over your current version; your downloads and settings are kept.

> Swoop isn't code-signed yet, so Edge may say *"isn't commonly downloaded"* (click **⋯ → Keep → Show more → Keep anyway**) and Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* (click **More info → Run anyway**).

### ✨ New

*(1.0.17's installer failed to build on GitHub, so its features ship in 1.0.18. Release builds now retry once.)*

- **Clear a whole section at once.** Right-click a section in the sidebar (**Needs attention**, **Completed**, **Videos**, …) and choose **Delete all…**. You confirm first, and the downloaded files stay on your computer unless you tick the box to delete them too.
- **Try all again.** Right-click **Needs attention** to retry every download that failed.
